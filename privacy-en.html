Privacy Policy

App: "Shopping Manager" · Last updated: October 2026

1. What data we collect

The "Shopping Manager" app does not collect or share any personal data with third parties. All shopping lists, stores, and reminders are stored only on your device.

2. Location

The app uses your location only to remind you about purchases when you are near a store. Your location never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any servers.

3. Microphone and Speech Recognition

The voice input feature allows you to add purchases by speaking them aloud. Speech processing is performed by iOS (Speech framework). Audio recordings are not saved or transmitted.

4. Camera

The camera is used only to scan product barcodes. Images are not saved or transmitted.

5. Notifications

The app sends local notifications (purchase reminders, nearby store reminders). Notifications are generated on the device and do not use external servers.

6. Third-party services

The app does not use third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, or data collection.

7. Artificial Intelligence (AI Assistant)

Future versions of the app plan to include an AI Assistant feature — a smart helper for creating lists, recipes, and purchase recommendations. This feature will be paid (via subscription).

When using the AI Assistant, text queries (not personal data) may be sent to secure servers of third-party AI providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic) solely to generate a response. Shopping lists, contacts, location, and other personal data are not transmitted.

You may decline to use the AI Assistant — all other app features work without it.

8. Purchases and Subscriptions

The app may offer paid subscriptions (e.g., access to the AI Assistant). Payment is processed through the App Store (Apple In-App Purchase). The app does not receive your bank card details — all payment processing is handled by Apple.

You can manage your subscription in your Apple ID settings.

9. Your Rights

You can delete all data at any time by deleting the app from your device. All data is stored locally and is removed with the app.
